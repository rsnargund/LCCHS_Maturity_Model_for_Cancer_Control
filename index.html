About the maturity framework Purpose, maturity levels, test sets, and how to explore

What this visual representation shows

Five capabilities that support the development of a systems approach to realising cancer control in health systems: Understanding, Data, Investment, Resources, and Execution.

Who this framework is for

Organizations participating in cancer control who seek to mature capabilities using a systems approach.

Levels of maturation

1 Initial 2 Managed 3 Defined 4 Quantitatively Managed 5 Optimizing

Tested characteristics are grouped into components of maturity within each capability, which simplifies understanding.

Intended use

This page is an interactive visual representation of the maturity model. It is not an assessment tool and does not score or rate organizations. It is intended to support visualization of the framework, to invite conversation about the capabilities and components, and to provide definitions of key terms.

Test sets comprise questions that can guide the maturation of capabilities required for cancer control. The test set questions and characteristics at each maturity level are presented in Appendix A and are shown here for reference and discussion.

How to explore

Click a capability circle to fan out its components. Capabilities stay open as you add others; click again to collapse. Click the center circle to clear everything.
